# Stratagem specification

WoW Forever leveling guide. Target client: `D:\WoW\World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_`, interface 16001 (listed first in `Stratagem.toc`). A route is a normal zone quest list. Quests that reward a best-in-slot piece for the selected spec are marked, and any such quest the route left out is inserted.

## Player-facing behavior

- Window: left categories, center guide list, right detail. Dark navy panels, violet actions, gold labels. Categories: All guides, Leveling, Professions, Reputation, Exploration, Legacy, Collectibles, Dungeons, Favorites, Options.
- Guides are grouped by bracket and zone (Horde and Alliance, 1–30 and 30–40, plus the other categories).
- Detail shows the zone, level range, faction, step count, progress, Continue, Open in tab, and Open in new tab. Each guide row has an Open button. The first visible guide is selected so Continue is available without an extra click. Continue opens the guide and sets the waypoint. Open in tab replaces the current guide tab. Open in new tab adds one, up to four.
- Until a guide has been started, login opens the window. The tracker shows Choose a guide instead of step buttons. Prev, Next, Done, and Point open that window when nothing is selected.
- Each quest is accept, complete, then turn in. Travel and "level toward" notes are optional and do not count. Progress is per character.
- Spec is chosen in the sidebar or with `/sg spec <key>`. Keys match NaowhForever_BiS (`arms-warrior`, `feral-dps-druid`, `feral-tank-druid`, …). Auto-detect uses the classic talent tab. Feral tank shares tab 2 with feral DPS and stays a manual choice.
- Wrong class, race, or faction is never shown. "Only quests that are BiS for my spec" hides rewards ranked for other specs. A journal BiS quest that the top-10 ranking slice names for nobody stays visible.
- On a leveling route, a quest that ranks for the player's class and is missing from that zone's written steps is inserted at its level. The accept, complete, and turn-in steps are one chain. A gold bar and the label Route BiS mean the quest was already on the guide and it upgrades the selected spec. A violet bar and the label Added BiS mean the chain was inserted for that spec. Switching spec changes the badges, not the step list. Dungeon quests are inserted only into a segment named for that dungeon.
- Horde 1–30 includes Camp Narache & Mulgore (tauren) and Valley of Trials & Durotar (orc and troll). Those steps are starting-zone quests. Their rewards are not best in slot, and the spec filter does not hide them. Quest ids come from the Forever Codex hub lists for those zones. They are not rows in `Data\Quests.lua`. The one Durotar BiS quest, shaman Call of Fire (1527), stays on that guide. The Broodmother (96261) stays on the 30–40 guide. Mulgore pins use classic map 1412. Forever redrew that zone, so a pin can sit off the camp. Durotar still uses its classic map.
- TomTom gets a waypoint when the step has a map id. The HUD arrow still shows. It points at the step and prints the distance in yards. Distance uses HereBeDragons or `C_Map.GetWorldPosFromMapPos` when one of those is present. On the same map, without either, it estimates 42 yards per map percent. `/sg arrowflip` reverses the arrow. A step with no coordinates does not raise the arrow.
- Slash: `/stratagem` and `/sg` with show, hide, tracker, next, prev, done, waypoint, spec, status, reset, reset all, arrowflip. Keybind: Toggle Stratagem.
- Saved variables: `StratagemDB` (account), `StratagemCharDB` (character).

## Data

- Live source, when loaded: `_G.NaowhForever` or `_G.NaowwahForever`, `Journal.BiSQuestData` plus `BiSData` spec rankings. Dungeon tags are copied from the snapshot onto live rows by quest id.
- Bundled snapshot: `Data\Quests.lua` and `Data\ItemSpecs.lua`, generated by `Tools\build_catalog.ps1` from the local Naowh journal and BiS list. 131 quests, about level 6 through 34. Higher brackets only contain quests that exist in that range. No quest id is invented.
- Routes live in `Guides.lua`. Dynamic dungeon, zone, class, and choice guides are built at login from the catalog.
- A segment may also carry `plain` quests. Engine marks those steps `path`. They are filtered by class, `notClass`, and race, then shown even when "only my spec" is on. The accept text says the reward is not best in slot, unless that quest id is in the catalog and ranked for the selected spec.

## Files the client loads

`Stratagem.toc`, `Bindings.xml`, `Core.lua`, `Specs.lua`, `Data\Quests.lua`, `Data\ItemSpecs.lua`, `BiS.lua`, `Guides.lua`, `Engine.lua`, `Nav.lua`, `UI\Theme.lua`, `UI\Widgets.lua`, `UI\Minimap.lua`, `UI\Tracker.lua`, `UI\Window.lua`, `Media\`.

## Current limits

The 30–40 routes are thin because the snapshot ends near level 34. Steps with coordinates in the text but no map id do not drop a TomTom pin. This build has not been loaded in the client yet.

## How to test this version

On the Forever character screen, enable Stratagem. In game:

1. `/sg` opens the window. Esc, the minimap button, and `/sg hide` close it.
2. The footer spec matches the character. Pick another spec for that class, `/reload`, and confirm it persisted. `/sg spec` prints the key.
3. Horde or Alliance 1–30 lists zone guides for your faction. A class-only guide you cannot do is absent. On a tauren, Camp Narache & Mulgore is in the list and Valley of Trials is absent unless you are a shaman (then only Call of Fire). On an orc or troll, Valley of Trials & Durotar is in the list and Mulgore is absent. Undead still get Tirisfal, not those two.
4. Continue on a zone guide shows accept / complete / turn-in steps, a progress bar, and reward names once the client knows the items.
5. Accepting a listed quest marks the accept step done when auto-advance is on.
6. Continue raises the HUD arrow. It points along the step and shows a yard distance. `/sg arrowflip` reverses it. With TomTom on, a pin is dropped as well and the HUD arrow stays.
7. `/sg status` names the live journal or the bundled snapshot.

## Repository

Each completed prompt is one commit, then a push to `origin` `main` on `https://github.com/hydrolive/stratagem.git`. No force-push. The Forever AddOns copy is a separate step and is not the git push.

## Last change

Leveling routes keep their written quest order. A quest that ranks for your class is inserted into that zone's guide when the route did not already list it. Chains already on the guide show a gold bar and Route BiS. Inserted chains show a violet bar and Added BiS. The HUD arrow points at the current step and shows the distance in yards, whether or not TomTom is enabled.

`/reload`. Open a zone guide and press Continue. Walk. The arrow should turn as you turn, and the yard number should fall as you approach. A spec upgrade that was not written into the route appears as Added BiS. One that was already listed appears as Route BiS. Change spec in the sidebar and the badges follow the new spec. `/sg arrowflip` if the arrow points backward.
