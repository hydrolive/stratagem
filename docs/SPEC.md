# Stratagem specification

WoW Forever leveling guide. Target client: `D:\WoW\World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_`, interface 16001 (listed first in `Stratagem.toc`). Leveling is one route for the character's faction. It follows the character's level, names every reward worth equipping, and marks gold only when that item is the best piece for the slot at the current level. The endgame top-10 rank does not set that badge.

## Player-facing behavior

- Window: left categories, center steps or a guide list, right detail. Dark navy panels, violet actions, gold labels. Categories, in order: Leveling, Professions, Reputation, Exploration, Legacy, Collectibles, Dungeons, Favorites, Level 60 BiS, Attunements, Options. There is no All guides category. A saved `all` category becomes Leveling.
- Leveling opens the faction route immediately. It does not ask the player to pick a zone guide. The step list is grouped by zone or dungeon. The same name appears again when the route returns. A tauren's first group is Camp Narache & Mulgore. An orc or troll's first group is Valley of Trials & Durotar. An undead character's starter is the Tirisfal catalog step that exists, not an invented Deathknell path. A night elf starts in Teldrassil. A dwarf or gnome starts in Dun Morogh. A human has no starter writeup, so the Alliance route begins at the first catalog quest. Each step still shows its level.
- The route is the starter path, then the catalog quests from the old zone guides, plus the journal chain that leads to each of those quests. A dungeon segment also takes that dungeon's journal quest list and those chains. Chain steps are required. They are grouped by the map where they start, so The Forgotten Pools stays in The Barrens and Nara Wildmane stays in Thunder Bluff, while Leaders of the Fang stays on Wailing Caverns. This is still not every side quest in a zone. A Barrens quest that does not lead to a catalog quest, and is not on the Wailing Caverns journal list, is not added. Outdoor quests are ordered by level. At the same level, a lead-in comes before a dungeon, then the better class-wide gear rank, then chain order. A chain quest uses its catalog level, then the journal row level, then the journal minimum, then Questie's quest level. If none of those exist, it uses the chain's minimum. A listed level below that quest's own journal minimum is raised to the minimum. A lead-in whose catalog level is above its journal row is pulled down to that row, so a high catalog number cannot delay the dungeon. Travel is not treated as a cost. Once a zone is chosen, the visit also takes that zone's quests within 3 levels, then the route leaves. A later quest there is a return visit, and the travel line says so. Barrens, Silverpine Forest, and Hillsbrad Foothills can therefore appear more than once.
- A dungeon is scheduled at the low end of its journal bracket (Ruins of Lordaeron 15, Wailing Caverns 17, Ragefire Chasm 13), raised only when a real lead-in is higher than that bracket. Every step in that block shows that level, not the catalog level. Within the block, quests stay in journal-row order. The steps are: accept each quest that starts outside, in that order; then accept quests that start inside, pinned at the journal entrance; then one Run step whose pin is that entrance; then turn in each quest in the same order. The turn-in pin is the journal turn-in when one exists. The Run step is finished only when every quest on it is turned in or its objectives are done. Quests ranked for another spec stay on the route. With "Hide rewards meant for other specs" on, the Run step does not wait for those. A quest the journal lists only as a follow-up of another quest in that dungeon (In Nightmares after The Glowing Shard, Returning the Lost Satchel) is not accepted before the clear. It is accept, complete, turn in after the turn-ins. Farms still follow that whole segment, once.
- Zephras Isle is the skyborne island. Skyborne characters take that guide as their starter. A mainland race is not required to go. If the catalog has a quest there whose race mask matches them, one optional group is inserted after the starter. For a tauren that quest is Meddlesome Mages. The travel step names the ride: the zeppelin from Mulgore for Horde, the zeppelin from Dalaran for Alliance, and the same ride back. The arrow stays on the mainland route. Mark the group done to skip the island. Island quests with no race mask are not offered to mainland races. The numbers on the transport tuple are not a level requirement. Bloodhoof's caravan order is The Ravaged Caravan, The Venture Co., then Supervisor Fizsprocket. Journey into Thunder Bluff is the handoff after that.
- The first visit to a dungeon adds one optional step per equipment slot the class can use at that level. The step names the item, the boss, and the journal drop percent, and the detail pane shows the item the same way a quest reward does. Later visits to that dungeon do not repeat the list. Endgame rank is not a filter. Trash is named and has no percent, because the journal stores that as a sentinel. A missing percent says "no percent in the journal". If the class cannot equip anything the journal lists, or the journal is not loaded, one step says so. Nothing invents a boss, an item, or a rate. The equip slot comes from the client item record. Without that record the farm step cannot name a piece. Mark the step done to skip the farm. The quest log does not check a farm off. Dungeon quests share the bracket level, so the clear is one visit. A later return is a different level band, not the same quests split by catalog level.
- Quests that were written into a zone guide stay required. A catalog quest inserted because it ranks for this class, and was not already on that written route, is marked (Optional) on the title and in the step text. It still does not depend on the selected spec, the worn gear, or the player level, so the step indexes stay put. Switching spec only hides steps. The progress bar counts required steps.
- The arrow and the tracker land on the first unfinished quest, farm, or gear step whose level is within 2 of the character. Travel notes are not that landing spot. The optional Zephras detour is not that landing spot either. Aiming does not mark the earlier quests done, so the player can scroll back. Travel and notes before the first unfinished required step are marked done only after some quest on the route is already done. Gaining a level moves the cursor forward when the current step is finished or more than 2 levels behind. It does not move backward onto an unfinished step, and it stays put while the player has stepped back (hold).
- Login selects the faction route whenever Leveling is the open category, including after an old All guides save. The arrow follows that route, not a leftover dungeon or profession guide. The window opens on its own only when this character had no saved guide. Right-click the tracker or the HUD arrow to open the leveling steps. Clicking Leveling in the sidebar does the same and points the arrow there.
- Selecting a guide, or logging in with auto-advance on, reads completed quests and the quest log. Accept is done when the quest is in the log or turned in. Complete is done when the objectives are done. A dungeon Run step is done when every quest on it that is still shown has its objectives done, or is already turned in. Turn-in is done only after the hand-in. Turning one quest in does not finish the others. A failed quest stays open. Optional gear quests are included in that check. Farms are not. Chat reports a skip count only when the player selects a guide. Prev stays on the step it returns to until that guide is selected again. Old per-zone progress does not carry onto the new route id. Catch-up fills it from the quest log. When the route's step list changes, that character's checkmarks on the route are cleared once so the old indexes cannot land on a different quest. Catch-up fills them again from the quest log. The HUD and the detail pane list objective text from the quest log on a Complete step and on a Run step, including the client's `0/12` count when it provides one. Until the quest is in the log, the line says the objectives show up then. It does not invent a kill or a gather line. A Turn in step on the HUD shows the same reward row as the detail pane: icon, name, and Take · Level BiS, Take · Upgrade, or Skip. Mouse over the icon for the item tooltip.
- Spec is chosen in the sidebar or with `/sg spec <key>`. Keys match NaowhForever_BiS. Auto-detect uses the classic talent tab. Feral tank shares tab 2 with feral DPS and stays a manual choice.
- Wrong class, race, or faction is never shown. "Hide rewards meant for other specs" hides quests the endgame list ranked for a different spec. A journal quest that the top-10 ranking slice names for nobody stays visible. Starting-zone steps stay visible either way.
- Gear advice is separate from which steps exist. For every reward the character can equip, a step says to take it when it is better than the worn piece. An empty slot counts as item level 0. A choice quest names the one piece to pick and says what to skip. Gold and the label Level BiS mean that item is the best armor tier, and the highest item level of that tier, among rewards this character can obtain from quests at or below their level. Green and the label Upgrade mean it is still worth taking, and a stronger piece for that slot is already available. Mail is not hunter or shaman gear before level 40. Plate is not warrior or paladin gear before level 40. A quest the character cannot accept does not count. The endgame rank is not shown on a leveling step.
- Horde starter steps are the way out of the start zone. Quest ids come from the Forever Codex hub lists. They are not rows in `Data\Quests.lua`. Reward names come from QuestieDB when that addon is enabled. A tauren hunter on The Hunt Begins is told to choose the leather waist while that slot is empty and mail is unusable. Shaman Call of Fire (1527) stays available to a tauren from the Durotar writeup, because that guide is not the tauren starter. The journal chain for 1527 also includes 1524 at Kranal Fiss when that step is not already on the starter. An orc or troll takes Call of Fire inside the Durotar starter, in the written order, and that starter block is not expanded. Mulgore pins use classic map 1412. Forever redrew that zone, so a pin can sit off the camp. Durotar still uses its classic map. A follow-up that the journal lists only as a turn-in, such as the step after The Glowing Shard, is named from Questie when QuestieDB is enabled. Without a name from the journal or from Questie, that step is left off rather than labeled with a made-up title.
- Level 60 BiS sits above Options and opens its steps immediately. There is one required step for each equipment slot, in a fixed order, using rank 1 from the loaded Naowh list for the selected spec. The text says where that item comes from. A quest source names the quest and the place. A dungeon source names the boss, the dungeon, and the journal percent when one exists. The words "Buy it on the auction house" appear only when the source text itself says auction. A world drop says the list does not call it an auction listing. The next step is an optional enchant for that slot. Enchant advice is for the item currently equipped, from Naowh's enchant helper, and it says so. The first step and the detail line show the current gear score and the score of these rank-1 pieces. The ideal number can rise while item links are still loading. Without NaowhForever_BiS the steps say the list loads with that addon. No enchant, boss, or auction price is invented. Changing spec rewrites the item text and does not change the step count.
- Attunements is a list, not an auto-opened route. The guides are Onyxia Attunement for Alliance (journal quest 6502), Onyxia Attunement for Horde (6602), and Molten Core Attunement (7848). The steps are the journal prerequisite list, then the journal series, then that quest, with duplicates skipped. When the journal lists several quests as alternatives, one step names them and asks the player to complete one. A quest the journal does not list is not added. If the journal is missing, the guide is one note that says to enable NaowhForever_DungeonJournal. The faction filter hides the other side's Onyxia guide. Molten Core is both factions.
- The other categories stay lists. Each row has an Open button. Continue opens that guide and sets the waypoint. Open in tab replaces the current guide tab. Open in new tab adds one, up to four.
- TomTom gets a waypoint when the step has a map id. The HUD arrow still shows. It points at the tracker's current step, names that step, and prints the distance in yards. Prev, Next, Done, and a click on a step move it. Left-click drag moves the arrow, and the spot is remembered. Right-click opens the leveling route. Direction and distance come from one vector. With HereBeDragons, that is `GetWorldVector` from the player to the pin, the same call TomTom and RXP use, then the player's facing is subtracted. Without it, the client's world position is used with that same angle formula. On the same map, without either, it estimates 42 yards per map percent and treats east as +x and north as −map y. Walking the way the arrow points reduces the yard count. `/sg arrowflip` mirrors the arrow (left becomes right). It does not rotate it 180 degrees. A step with no coordinates does not raise the arrow. A Run step pins the journal entrance and super-tracks the first quest on that clear whose objectives are still open. Steps that share one camp pin keep the same direction until the route leaves that pin.
- Slash: `/stratagem` and `/sg` with show, hide, tracker, next, prev, done, waypoint, spec, status, reset, reset all, arrowflip. Keybind: Toggle Stratagem.
- Saved variables: `StratagemDB` (account), `StratagemCharDB` (character).

## Data

- Live source, when loaded: `_G.NaowhForever` or `_G.NaowwahForever`, `Journal.BiSQuestData` plus `BiSData` spec rankings. Dungeon tags are copied from the snapshot onto live rows by quest id. Level 60 sources, enchants, gear score, dungeon loot, and attunement chains are read from that addon at runtime. They are not copied into this repo.
- Bundled snapshot: `Data\Quests.lua` and `Data\ItemSpecs.lua`, generated by `Tools\build_catalog.ps1` from the local Naowh journal and BiS list. 131 quests, about level 6 through 34. Higher brackets only contain quests that exist in that range. No quest id, item id, boss name, or drop chance is invented.
- Zone writeups live in `Guides.lua` as sources. `Route.lua` weaves them into `route-h` and `route-a`. Profession, reputation, and dynamic guides are still compiled on their own.
- A segment may also carry `plain` quests. Engine marks those steps `path`. They are filtered by class, `notClass`, and race, then shown even when the spec filter is on. The accept text calls them starting-zone quests. `Gear.lua` appends the reward sentence once QuestieDB has been scanned. Without QuestieDB those steps cannot name a reward. Catalog quests still can, from the reward ids already on the quest row.
- Gear score uses `ns.NaowhScore` when Naowh is loaded. The current number is the player's score. The ideal number is the score of the rank-1 item links, falling back to the equipped item while a link is still loading. It is not the level-60 formula curve.

## Files the client loads

`Stratagem.toc`, `Bindings.xml`, `Core.lua`, `Specs.lua`, `Data\Quests.lua`, `Data\ItemSpecs.lua`, `BiS.lua`, `Gear.lua`, `Guides.lua`, `Route.lua`, `Engine.lua`, `Nav.lua`, `UI\Theme.lua`, `UI\Widgets.lua`, `UI\Minimap.lua`, `UI\Tracker.lua`, `UI\Window.lua`, `Media\`.

QuestieDB is an optional dependency. Stratagem reads `_G.ItemDB` and `_G.QuestDB` from it. It does not copy that database into this repo. NaowhForever, NaowhForever_BiS, and NaowhForever_DungeonJournal are optional. Without them, farms, Level 60 BiS, and attunements say what is missing.

## Current limits

The bundled catalog ends in the mid 30s. With the dungeon journal loaded, each catalog quest also brings the earlier quests in its journal chain, and a dungeon brings that dungeon's journal quest list. That is still not every side quest in The Barrens, Silverpine, or Hillsbrad, and it is not a route to 60. Without the journal, chain quests are absent, dungeon order falls back to catalog levels, and entrance pins fall back to the segment arrival pin. A chain step the journal does not name is added only when QuestieDB supplies the name. The Glowing Shard follow-up that has no journal name is one of those. Objective lines exist only after the client returns them. Level 60 BiS, enchant plans, auction prices, and attunement steps exist only when the matching Naowh addon is loaded. A world drop is not treated as an auction listing. Steps with coordinates in the text but no map id do not drop a TomTom pin. This build has not been loaded in the client yet.

## How to test this version

On the Forever character screen, enable Stratagem, NaowhForever_BiS, NaowhForever_DungeonJournal, and QuestieDB if they are installed. In game:

1. `/sg` opens the window on Leveling. The center pane is the step list, grouped by zone, with no guide picker and no All guides button. Esc, the minimap button, and `/sg hide` close it.
2. On a tauren the first group is Camp Narache & Mulgore. Supervisor Fizsprocket follows The Venture Co., and Journey into Thunder Bluff follows Fizsprocket. Zephras Isle, if it appears, is marked (Optional) and names the zeppelin from Mulgore. The arrow does not land on it. On an orc or troll the first group is Valley of Trials & Durotar. The next groups follow quest level. The Barrens should include The Forgotten Pools, The Stagnant Oasis, Altered Beings, Hamuul Runetotem, and Nara Wildmane, not only Centaur Bracers. A zone name can appear again later. Each step shows a level. The first login after this route change clears old route checkmarks. The quest log fills the ones you have already turned in.
3. The first Wailing Caverns group ends with lines marked (Optional). With the dungeon journal loaded, a line names the item, the boss, and a percent, and the detail pane shows that item. Trash has no percent. A later Wailing Caverns group does not repeat those lines. Mark done skips a farm. Without the journal, the line says the journal is required.
4. An inserted gear quest that was not on the written zone route is marked (Optional). A quest that was written into the zone guide, and a journal chain step that leads to one, is not.
5. On a tauren hunter who still has an empty waist, The Hunt Begins should say to choose the leather waist, mark it Level BiS in gold, and say to skip mail. On the Complete step the HUD lists the quest-log objectives and the count, and it advances when they are done. On the Turn in step the HUD shows the Nomadic Belt icon, the name, and Take · Level BiS. Mouse over the icon. Equip a better waist, or level past a better one, and the gold mark should drop.
6. The HUD arrow names the current step and shows a yard distance. Walk the way it points and the yards should fall. Next and Prev move it. Left-click drag moves it, and `/reload` keeps that spot. `/sg arrowflip` mirrors it. Right-click opens the leveling steps, and the arrow stays on that route. With TomTom on, a pin is dropped as well.
7. Ruins of Lordaeron comes before Wailing Caverns. The steps in each dungeon show the journal difficulty (15, then 17), not a descending catalog 22, 21, 20. Lead-ins such as The Forgotten Pools are finished before the accepts. The dungeon is every accept, then one Run step that lists each quest's objectives, then one turn-in per quest. In Nightmares comes after those turn-ins, not as its own clear in the middle.
8. Level 60 BiS, above Options, opens slot steps without a second click. With Naowh loaded, each slot names a rank-1 item and a source. A world drop does not say to buy it. The enchant line is marked (Optional) and talks about the equipped item. The detail line shows a gear score now and an ideal after the pieces. Without Naowh, the steps say the list loads with NaowhForever_BiS.
9. Attunements lists Onyxia for your faction and Molten Core. Open one. The steps match the journal chain. A quest you remember that the journal does not list is absent. Without the journal, the guide says to enable it.
10. `/sg status` names the live journal or the bundled snapshot. The footer spec matches the character. `/sg spec` prints the key, and `/reload` keeps a manual spec.

## Repository

Each completed prompt is one commit, then a push to `origin` `main` on `https://github.com/hydrolive/stratagem.git`. No force-push. The Forever AddOns copy is a separate step and is not the git push.

## Last change

A Complete step on the HUD lists the quest-log objectives and the live count, and it advances when they are done. A Turn in step shows the reward icon, the name, and Take · Level BiS or Take · Upgrade. The arrow's direction and the yard count now come from one world vector, so walking toward the arrow reduces the distance. `/sg arrowflip` still mirrors it. Dungeons are ordered by the journal bracket, so Ruins of Lordaeron (15) comes before Wailing Caverns (17). Each dungeon is every accept, one Run step with every objective, then one turn-in at a time. Lead-ins are finished first. Follow-ups such as In Nightmares come after the turn-ins. A changed route clears old route checkmarks once.

`/reload`. On The Hunt Begins, the Complete step should show the kill or gather lines and move on at the last count. The Turn in step should show the Nomadic Belt icon. Walk toward the arrow and confirm the yards fall. Scroll to the dungeons and confirm Ruins of Lordaeron is a single accept, Run, turn-in block before Wailing Caverns, with the lead-in quests earlier.
