# Stratagem specification

WoW Forever gear guide. Target client: `D:\WoW\World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_`, interface 16001 (listed first in `Stratagem.toc`). Gear is one list for the character's faction. It shows quest rewards, dungeon drops, crafts, and world drops that beat what you already have for the selected spec, within 3 levels. It does not choose a leveling path. Level BiS is the best piece for that spec you can wear at the current level. Upgrade is still worth taking. The endgame top-10 rank does not set that badge. A higher item level is not enough.

## Player-facing behavior

- Window: left categories, center steps or a guide list, right detail. Dark navy panels, violet actions, gold labels. Each list has a scrollbar on the right. Drag the thumb, or click the track, to move. The mouse wheel still scrolls. The bar sits above the rows so a row does not take the click. Categories, in order: Gear, Professions, Reputation, Exploration, Legacy, Collectibles, Dungeons, Favorites, Level 60 BiS, Attunements, Options. There is no All guides category. A saved `all` category becomes Gear. The category id is still `leveling`, so an older save opens Gear.
- Gear opens that faction's upgrade list immediately. It does not ask the player to pick a zone, and it does not walk accept, complete, and turn-in steps. The player chooses where to level. Four tabs filter that list: Quests, Profession, Auction House, and World Drops. The choice is saved on the account. Each line is one optional pickup. A line shows only when that item beats what the character already has equipped or in their bags for the selected spec, and the pickup is within 3 levels. Equipped gear and bags 0 through 4 count. The bank does not. An empty slot counts. The same item level is not an upgrade. A higher item level with the wrong stats for the spec is left off. Cloth does not replace leather, mail, or plate. For rings and trinkets, a new piece has to beat the worse of the two you would wear. One owned ring or trinket still leaves the other slot open. If the character already has a better piece, already has that item, or has turned the quest in, the line is marked done and left off. A Done click stays done across tab changes. Changing gear, bags, professions, or level runs that check again. The first line, when the selected tab has nothing, says so for that tab. Items whose stats are not cached yet stay off until the client loads them.
- A quest line is added only when the catalog, QuestieDB, or the journal chain names that quest, and at least one reward is gear this class can wear. The pin comes from the catalog, or from the journal start when the catalog has no pin. Reward ids come from the catalog and from QuestieDB item quest rewards. A Naowh source that points at a quest is included the same way. A quest with no name is left off. The line's level is the catalog level, then the Questie quest level. If neither names a level, the line stays at 1.
- Dungeon lines come from the journal loot table, one step per item this class can wear. The text names the boss and the drop percent. Trash has no percent. A missing percent says "no percent in the journal". The level is the journal bracket, or higher when the item cannot be worn yet. The pin is the dungeon entrance. Without the journal, those lines are absent. Nothing invents a boss, an item, or a rate.
- Craft lines come from the loaded recipe list, and from a Naowh source whose text says Crafted. The profession name comes from the profession spell, or from the source text before the middle dot. The note names the skill, the trainer, the vendor, or the first drop mob only when the recipe names them. If the profession is missing, the line says the list does not name it. A craft that is also a quest reward stays on the quest line. Numbers on a drop tuple are not used as coordinates. The Profession tab lists crafts from professions the character has, including bind-on-pickup and bind-on-equip. The profession match is the skill line from the client, the same id the recipe list uses. A short skill still shows the line, and the note still says what the recipe asks for. A bind-on-pickup craft from a profession the character does not have is left off. A source that only says Crafted, with no profession id, is not treated as the character's profession.
- World-drop and auction lines come from the loaded Naowh source list. Bind type comes only from the client item record: unbound and bind-on-equip can be bought from another player. Bind-on-pickup, bind-on-use, and an unknown bind are not put on the Auction House. The addon does not guess a bind type or an auction price, and it does not read the letters boe out of a source string. Auction House shows bind-on-equip crafts from professions the character does not have, plus world drops the client marks unbound or bind-on-equip. A source that itself says auction stays on that tab even when the bind is not loaded yet. A bind-on-equip world drop is listed on both Auction House and World Drops, and both show the farm line. World Drops also lists dungeon drops, under the dungeon name, with the boss and the journal percent. Trash still has no percent. The same item is not repeated when it is already a quest, dungeon, or craft step.
- A world drop names the mob with the highest percent in the QuestieDB wowhead drop list, that percent to one decimal, and about how many kills that percent takes. The kill count is `ceil(100 / chance)` from the real percent. The line then says how many of that mob Stratagem has recorded for this character. The count starts when the addon is recording. It is not a lifetime total from before that. A kill counts when the combat log says the player or the player's pet made the killing blow. If that drop list has no percent, the line says so. No mob, percent, or old kill total is invented.
- Wrong class, race, or faction is left off. Mail is not a hunter or shaman upgrade before level 40. Plate is not a warrior or paladin upgrade before level 40. The endgame rank is not a filter. Switching spec can change which piece a choice quest names. It does not add a quest the class cannot wear.
- The arrow and the tracker land on the first listed upgrade that is not done. Gaining a level or changing gear refreshes which lines are visible. It does not mark the remaining lines done. A line more than 3 levels out stays hidden and is not marked done. Stepping back with Prev stays put until Next or Done.
- Login selects the faction gear list whenever Gear is the open category, including after an old All guides save. The arrow follows that list, not a leftover dungeon or profession guide. The window opens on its own only when this character had no saved guide. Right-click the tracker or the HUD arrow to open Gear. Clicking Gear in the sidebar does the same.
- Selecting a guide, or logging in with auto-advance on, reads completed quests and the quest log. Accept is done when the quest is in the log or turned in. Complete is done when the objectives are done. A dungeon Run step is done when every quest on it that is still shown has its objectives done, or is already turned in. Turn-in is done only after the hand-in. On Gear, a quest line is done only when that quest is turned in. Accepting it is not enough. A dungeon, craft, or world-drop line has no quest, so it closes when the worn piece is already better, when that item is in your bags, or when Done is clicked. Turning one quest in does not finish the others. A failed quest stays open. Optional gear quests are included in that check. Farms are not. Chat reports a skip count only when the player selects a guide. Prev stays on the step it returns to until that guide is selected again. Old per-zone progress does not carry onto the new route id. Catch-up fills it from the quest log. When the route's step list changes, that character's checkmarks on the route are cleared once so the old indexes cannot land on a different quest. Catch-up fills them again from the quest log. The HUD and the detail pane list every objective line from the quest log on a Complete step and on a Run step, including the client's `0/12` count when it provides one. There is no line cap. The tracker grows with those lines, and the mouse wheel scrolls them when the card would be taller than the screen. The detail pane gives that text all the room above the buttons and any reward rows that are showing, and the mouse wheel scrolls the rest. Until the quest is in the log, the line says the objectives show up then. It does not invent a kill or a gather line. A quest that continues earlier quests shows a chain marker on the tracker, on its step row, and on the detail title. Mouse over the marker for those earlier quests, in order. The first quest in a chain has no marker. A dungeon Run step's marker lists each chained quest on that clear and the quests that lead to it. A Turn in step on the HUD shows the same reward row as the detail pane: icon, name, and Take · Level BiS, Take · Upgrade, or Skip. Mouse over the icon for the item tooltip.
- Spec is chosen in the sidebar or with `/sg spec <key>`. Keys match NaowhForever_BiS. Auto-detect uses the classic talent tab. Feral tank shares tab 2 with feral DPS and stays a manual choice.
- Wrong class, race, or faction is never shown. "Hide rewards meant for other specs" hides outdoor quests the endgame list ranked for a different spec. It does not hide a pickup that is part of a dungeon clear. A journal quest that the top-10 ranking slice names for nobody stays visible. Gear still shows an upgrade this class can wear when that list ranked the quest for another spec.
- The Gear list is the upgrades for the selected spec. Stats that spec wants are read from the item once the client has cached it. Caster specs also count spell damage, spell healing, and spell power when the client reports them. Melee specs count attack power the same way. A missing stat adds nothing. Gold and the label Level BiS mean that item's spec score is at least as high as the other pieces you can wear now in that slot, and it beats what you already have. Green and the label Upgrade mean it still beats what you have, and a stronger piece for that slot is already available at your level. Mail is not hunter or shaman gear before level 40. Plate is not warrior or paladin gear before level 40. A quest the character cannot accept does not count. The endgame rank is not shown on a Gear line. Equipping a better piece, or already having that item in your bags, marks the line done and leaves it off. Take the piece off, or get rid of the bag item, and the line comes back. Turning the quest in stays done. The step ids are rebuilt when Questie reward data arrives, not on every equip. A Done click stays done. The progress count covers the selected tab. Lines on the other tabs stay off that count.
- Item names come from the client or from QuestieDB. If neither has a name, the line says item and the id. A quest, boss, vendor, recipe, or auction price is never invented. Zone writeups remain in Guides.lua and are not opened. The player chooses where to level.
- Level 60 BiS sits above Options and opens its steps immediately. There is one required step for each equipment slot, in a fixed order, using rank 1 from the loaded Naowh list for the selected spec. The text says where that item comes from. A quest source names the quest and the place. A dungeon source names the boss, the dungeon, and the journal percent when one exists. The words "Buy it on the auction house" appear only when the source text itself says auction. A world drop says the list does not call it an auction listing. A drop, an auction listing, or a quest reward shows that item's icon in the detail pane and on the tracker. The next step is an optional enchant for the rank 1 piece in that slot. The line names the enchant from the Naowh list, including head, shoulder, waist, and leg enchants when that item can take one. It does not depend on the item you are wearing. A slot with no enchant on that list says so. The first step and the detail line show the current gear score and the score of these rank-1 pieces. The ideal number can rise while item links are still loading. Without NaowhForever_BiS the steps say the list loads with that addon. No enchant, boss, or auction price is invented. Changing spec rewrites the item text and does not change the step count.
- Attunements is a list, not an auto-opened route. The guides are Onyxia Attunement for Alliance (journal quest 6502), Onyxia Attunement for Horde (6602), and Molten Core Attunement (7848). The steps are the journal prerequisite list, then the journal series, then that quest, with duplicates skipped. When the journal lists several quests as alternatives, one step names them and asks the player to complete one. A quest the journal does not list is not added. If the journal is missing, the guide is one note that says to enable NaowhForever_DungeonJournal. The faction filter hides the other side's Onyxia guide. Molten Core is both factions.
- The other categories stay lists. Each row has an Open button. Continue opens that guide and sets the waypoint. Open in tab replaces the current guide tab. Open in new tab adds one, up to four.
- TomTom gets a waypoint when the step has a map id. The HUD arrow still shows. It points at the tracker's current step, names that step, and prints the distance in yards. Prev, Next, Done, and a click on a step move it. Left-click drag moves the arrow, and the spot is remembered. Right-click opens Gear. Direction and distance come from one vector. With HereBeDragons, that is `GetWorldVector` from the player to the pin, the same call TomTom and RXP use, then the player's facing is subtracted. Without it, the client's world position is used with that same angle formula. On the same map, without either, it estimates 42 yards per map percent and treats east as +x and north as −map y. Walking the way the arrow points reduces the yard count. `/sg arrowflip` mirrors the arrow (left becomes right). It does not rotate it 180 degrees. A step with no coordinates does not raise the arrow. A Run step pins the journal entrance and super-tracks the first quest on that clear whose objectives are still open. Steps that share one camp pin keep the same direction until the route leaves that pin.
- Slash: `/stratagem` and `/sg` with show, hide, tracker, next, prev, done, waypoint, spec, status, reset, reset all, arrowflip. Keybind: Toggle Stratagem.
- Saved variables: `StratagemDB` (account, including the Gear tab) and `StratagemCharDB` (character, including recorded kill counts).

## Data

- Live source, when loaded: `_G.NaowhForever` or `_G.NaowwahForever`, `Journal.BiSQuestData` plus `BiSData` spec rankings. Dungeon tags are copied from the snapshot onto live rows by quest id. Level 60 sources, enchants, gear score, dungeon loot, and attunement chains are read from that addon at runtime. They are not copied into this repo.
- Bundled snapshot: `Data\Quests.lua` and `Data\ItemSpecs.lua`, generated by `Tools\build_catalog.ps1` from the local Naowh journal and BiS list. 131 quests, about level 6 through 34. Higher brackets only contain quests that exist in that range. No quest id, item id, boss name, or drop chance is invented.
- Zone writeups live in Guides.lua as source data. They are not opened. Gear.lua builds gear-h and gear-a in the leveling category. Profession, reputation, exploration, dungeon, and the other dynamic guides are still compiled on their own. Level 60 BiS stays guide bis-60.
- A segment may also carry `plain` quests. Engine marks those steps `path`. They are filtered by class, `notClass`, and race, then shown even when the spec filter is on. The accept text calls them starting-zone quests. `Gear.lua` appends the reward sentence once QuestieDB has been scanned. Without QuestieDB those steps cannot name a reward. Catalog quests still can, from the reward ids already on the quest row. A catalog entry may be `{ id, class, races }` instead of a bare id. Compile copies the catalog row and applies that class or race. `races = false` clears the mask. The catalog row itself is not edited.
- Gear score uses `ns.NaowhScore` when Naowh is loaded. The current number is the player's score. The ideal number is the score of the rank-1 item links, falling back to the equipped item while a link is still loading. It is not the level-60 formula curve.

## Files the client loads

`Stratagem.toc`, `Bindings.xml`, `Core.lua`, `Specs.lua`, `Data\Quests.lua`, `Data\ItemSpecs.lua`, `BiS.lua`, `Gear.lua`, `Guides.lua`, `Route.lua`, `Engine.lua`, `Nav.lua`, `UI\Theme.lua`, `UI\Widgets.lua`, `UI\Minimap.lua`, `UI\Tracker.lua`, `UI\Window.lua`, `Media\`.

QuestieDB is an optional dependency. Stratagem reads `_G.ItemDB` and `_G.QuestDB` from it. It does not copy that database into this repo. NaowhForever, NaowhForever_BiS, NaowhForever_DungeonJournal, and NaowhForever_Discovery are optional. Without the first three, farms, Level 60 BiS, and attunements say what is missing. Discovery is read at runtime from `CompletoQuestData` and is not copied into this repo. Without it, a lead-in the journal does not name, including The Barrens Oases, is left off.

## Current limits

Gear can only name a quest reward the catalog or QuestieDB lists, a dungeon drop the journal lists, a craft the recipe list or a Crafted source lists, or a world drop or auction line on the loaded Naowh source list. It does not choose where to level. A piece more than 3 levels ahead stays hidden. Without QuestieDB, quests that are not in the catalog cannot name a reward and are left off, and a world drop says the drop list has no percent. Without the dungeon journal, dungeon drops are absent. Without the recipe list, crafts come only from source lines that say Crafted. The Dungeons category is still its own list of catalog dungeon quests. Level 60 BiS, enchant plans, auction prices, and attunement steps exist only when the matching Naowh addon is loaded. Bind type is whatever the client reports. An unknown bind is not listed on the Auction House. Tracked kills are only the kills Stratagem has recorded for this character. Steps with coordinates in the text but no map id do not drop a TomTom pin. This build has not been loaded in the client yet.

## How to test this version

On the Forever character screen, enable Stratagem, NaowhForever_BiS, NaowhForever_DungeonJournal, NaowhForever_Discovery, and QuestieDB if they are installed. In game:

1. `/sg` opens the window on Gear. The center pane has Quests, Profession, Auction House, and World Drops. There is no zone route and no All guides button. Esc, the minimap button, and `/sg hide` close it. Switching tabs keeps a Done click done.
2. A line shows only when that item beats what you have equipped or in your bags for the selected spec, within 3 levels. An empty slot counts. A higher item level with the wrong stats does not. Equip a better piece and the line leaves. Turn the quest in and its line leaves. Accepting the quest does not. If the item was only in your bags, getting rid of it brings the line back. The bank does not count.
3. World Drops lists world drops and dungeon drops. With the dungeon journal loaded, a dungeon line names the item, the boss, and a percent. Trash has no percent. Without the journal, those lines are absent. Nothing guesses a boss or a rate.
4. Profession lists crafts from professions you have. A bind-on-pickup recipe from a profession you do not have stays off. Auction House lists bind-on-equip crafts from professions you do not have, and bind-on-equip world drops. A world drop names the percent, about how many kills that takes, and how many of that mob this character has been recorded killing. If the drop list has no percent, the line says so. The kill count is not a lifetime total from before Stratagem was recording.
5. On an empty slot the line can say Level BiS in gold. When a stronger piece for that slot is already available at your level, it says Upgrade in green. The detail pane and the tracker show the item icon. Mouse over the icon.
6. The HUD arrow names the current step and shows a yard distance. Walk the way it points and the yards should fall. Next and Prev move it. Left-click drag moves it, and `/reload` keeps that spot. `/sg arrowflip` mirrors it. Right-click opens Gear. With TomTom on, a pin is dropped as well.
7. When the selected tab has nothing in the next 3 levels, the first line says so for that tab. Gain a level, learn a profession, or take off a better piece, and a line can come back. A line more than 3 levels ahead stays hidden and is not marked done. A Done click stays done.
8. Level 60 BiS, above Options, opens slot steps without a second click. With Naowh loaded, each slot names a rank-1 item and a source. A world drop does not say to buy it. A drop, auction item, or quest reward shows its icon. Each enchant line names the enchant for that rank 1 piece, or says the list has none. The detail line shows a gear score now and an ideal after the pieces. Without Naowh, the steps say the list loads with NaowhForever_BiS.
9. Attunements lists Onyxia for your faction and Molten Core. Open one. The steps match the journal chain. A quest you remember that the journal does not list is absent. Without the journal, the guide says to enable it.
10. `/sg status` names the live journal or the bundled snapshot. The footer spec matches the character. `/sg spec` prints the key, and `/reload` keeps a manual spec.

## Repository

Each completed prompt is one commit, then a push to `origin` `main` on `https://github.com/hydrolive/stratagem.git`. No force-push. The Forever AddOns copy is a separate step and is not the git push.
